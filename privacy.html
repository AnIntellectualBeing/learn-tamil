Privacy Policy

Application: Learn Tamil: Spoken Survival • Package Name: com.anintellectualcompany.learntamil • Effective Date: August 31, 2026 • Developer: An Intellectual Company

1. Overview

Learn Tamil: Spoken Survival ("the App", "we", "us") is committed to respecting and protecting user privacy. This policy explains our data practices for the Android application.

2. Data Collection & Permissions

A. Offline First

The core audio lessons and vocabulary in Learn Tamil run locally on your device. We do not require account registration or login to use the learning features.

B. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays non-intrusive advertisements served by Google AdMob to keep the application free. Google AdMob may collect coarse device parameters and the Google Advertising ID (AD_ID) in compliance with Google Play Policies.

C. Diagnostics & Analytics

Anonymous diagnostic metrics and crash reports may be processed to identify and resolve software errors.

3. Permissions Used

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required for loading audio updates and AdMob advertisements.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Verifies network status.

4. Data Deletion & Contact

Since the app functions without requiring user accounts, all learning progress is stored locally on your device and can be wiped instantly by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

For any questions or data requests, contact us at: anintellectualcompany@gmail.com.
