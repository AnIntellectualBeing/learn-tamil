Privacy Policy

Application: Learn Tamil Survive Chennai • Effective Date: August 31, 2026 • Developer: An Intellectual Company

1. Introduction

Welcome to Learn Tamil Survive Chennai. We are committed to protecting your privacy and keeping our practices simple and transparent. This policy explains what information we process when you use our mobile application.

2. What Information We Collect

We do not require account registration or collect personal personal details (such as names or phone numbers) to use the core app.

  • Offline & Local Data: All phrase lessons, audio clips, and saved bookmarks remain stored directly on your device.
  • Crash Reports & Performance: We may receive anonymous technical diagnostic reports to help us fix crashes and improve app speed.
  • Advertising: The free version displays standard advertisements served by Google AdMob. Google AdMob uses non-personal device advertising identifiers in compliance with Google Play policies.

3. Device Features We Use

  • Internet Connection: Used to stream audio updates and load advertisements.
  • Audio Playback: Used to play native voice pronunciation when you tap a phrase.

4. Data Control & Deletion

Since we do not store personal account data on cloud servers, all your saved favorites and progress are stored entirely on your phone. You can erase all local data anytime by clearing the app's cache/storage in your phone settings or by uninstalling the app.
