Data Deletion Policy

Learn Tamil: Spoken Survival (com.anintellectualcompany.learntamil) does not maintain online accounts or store sensitive personal identifiers on remote servers.

How to delete your local app data:

1. Open your Android device Settings ➡️ Apps ➡️ Learn Tamil ➡️ Storage.
2. Tap Clear Storage / Clear Data.
3. All local favorites and history will be permanently deleted.
