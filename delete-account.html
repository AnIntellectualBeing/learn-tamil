Data Deletion Policy

Learn Tamil Survive Chennai does not require an online account or store personal identifiable information on external cloud servers.

How to wipe all local data:

1. Open your Android device Settings ➡️ Apps ➡️ Learn Tamil ➡️ Storage.
2. Tap Clear Storage / Clear Data.
3. All local favorites and history will be deleted instantly.
